## Outcome

The contributor guide states that submitting original work confirms the contributor has the right to submit it under the project's MIT terms, while the contributor retains copyright. It requires provenance and applicable terms for incorporated third-party material, preservation of upstream notices, and excludes unclear-rights material and data without redistribution permission. It requires Conventional Commits for task-owned commits but no DCO or CLA. The PR template prompts for provenance when applicable without adding a rights-signoff checkbox.

The license policy and M00 task definitions match that process. The dependency policy distinguishes current bootstrap/build-input review from future production admission; unselected candidates remain deferred until a task proposes the concrete component/version/use and distribution route.
