## Deliverables

- Realm/endpoint/transport/lifetime flow keys, worker dispatch and TCP/UDP lifetime handling.
- Accounting for Rust/native flow allocations, held packets, queues and explicit admission/pressure reasons.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Opposite directions share a worker even when NIC RSS is asymmetric; different realms and reused tuples do not share stale decisions.
- [ ] Borrowed ring or packet memory never outlives its adapter loan and no Rust unwind crosses native boundaries.
- [ ] Admission/queue limits remain bounded and cannot silently evict active blocked state then allow that same connection.

## Required tests and validation

- Native adapter lab plus portable synthetic dispatch: send bidirectional tuples across workers, VLAN/realm collisions, TCP tuple reuse and UDP expiry; assert independent expected ownership.
- Feed midstream, reordered, truncated and unsupported migration cases; verify declared state/visibility rather than invented complete history.
- Offer excess flows and queue pressure while a blocked connection remains active; measure full native/Rust peak and confirm subsequent packets remain blocked or receive explicit proven safety behavior.
