## Deliverables

- Nonblocking bounded event exporter with boot/worker/sequence identities, deduplication and gap counters.
- Activity daemon single SQLite writer with patched pinned baseline, bounded reads/WAL/ingestion and no payload capture.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Records separate policy verdict from adapter-confirmed action and preserve absence/incomplete-detail reasons.
- [ ] Queues/native datagram limits and aggregation preserve bounded metadata, exact counters and visible collection completeness.
- [ ] Unavailable/full/corrupt activity storage cannot delay lookup/forward/drop or disable a healthy engine.

## Required tests and validation

- Pinned FreeBSD IPC/SQLite lab: ingest synthetic real-engine events, duplicates, gaps and over-envelope detail; compare global counters, primary reason and completeness intervals.
- Attempt malicious/unknown event fields, excessive detail, invalid peers and repeated aggregation across differing outcome/profile/reason keys; verify bounds and separation.
- Stop activity, fill disk and pressure writer/queues during traffic; observe continued enforcement, counted drops and recovery without claiming exactly-once or zero reporting loss.
