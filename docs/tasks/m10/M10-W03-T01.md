## Deliverables

- At-least-five non-developer participant criteria and timed task/error/assistance protocol.
- Category profile/assignment and reason/narrow-exception task scripts with independent expected outcomes.
- Draft/applied/bypass/visibility comprehension probes plus locale/accessibility/state sampling.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At least five participants who have not developed Detew are required; at least four independently create/assign a category profile within ten minutes after readiness.
- [ ] At least four independently find a block reason and add a narrow exception within three minutes; timing and success checks are specified before sessions.
- [ ] Installation/download time and assistance/mistakes are recorded separately from measured task times; coached results are not counted as independent successes.
- [ ] Participants must understand draft versus enforced change, bypass, and metadata versus decrypted content; a misleading interface fails despite fast completion.
- [ ] The protocol includes English/pt-BR and relevant narrow/keyboard/error/stale/conflict states without inventing a new numeric demographic/retention target.

## Required tests and validation

- Independent protocol review: run a synthetic dry-run and check timer start/stop, exception scope/guardrail expectations, assistance marking and separate installation measurements.
- Use truthful and intentionally misleading draft/stale/bypass screenshots/state fixtures to validate comprehension questions do not reveal the expected answer.
- Audit participant independence/consent and public redaction requirements against the interview/pilot protocol; actual study sessions remain blocked on technically ready integrated candidate.
