## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Report contains at least 10 interviewees, five independent sites after founder environment and five non-developer study participants with distinct denominators and actual assistance/failure counts.
- [ ] Timed success/comprehension targets, actual install/update/recovery outcomes and technical/corpus results are linked to accepted candidate versions.
- [ ] The declared pilot period and repeat-use/keep-remove findings are reported without an invented retention threshold or market-size extrapolation.
- [ ] No browsing records, secrets, identifying site context or sensitive raw captures enter the public dossier; negative evidence remains visible through redacted summaries.
- [ ] Open defects, lawful source maintenance, attachment reliability and alternatives/friction results receive explicit stop-condition dispositions.
