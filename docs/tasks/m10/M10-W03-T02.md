## Deliverables

- At-least-five participant timing/success/mistake/assistance and comprehension report.
- Evidence of independently correct category profile/assignment and narrow exception results.
- Reviewed usability defects and repeated affected-session evidence after changes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At least five non-developer participants complete sessions; at least four independently create and assign a category profile within ten minutes after installation readiness.
- [ ] At least four independently find the deciding reason and create a narrow exception within three minutes; guardrails still apply and enforcement scope is verified.
- [ ] Installation/download time, errors and assistance are reported separately with failed and abandoned sessions in denominators.
- [ ] Participants correctly distinguish draft/applied/bypass status and limited metadata visibility; falsely understood enforcement/decryption remains a G2 blocker despite time compliance.
- [ ] English/pt-BR, narrow triage, keyboard/focus and failure-state observations are recorded on actual supported host/browser builds; polished screenshots cannot replace interaction evidence.

## Required tests and validation

- Consented real native host with accepted candidate and synthetic activity: conduct predeclared sessions using independent observers/timers and no coaching; verify saved/applied revisions, assignments and actual exception scope.
- Introduce controlled draft, stale refresh, bypass and reduced encrypted-visibility conditions; collect explanation in participant language and score against the predeclared rubric.
- Replay failed/ambiguous tasks after targeted repairs using a documented unbiased repeat/new-participant strategy; retain original results, affected revision and separate assistance records.
