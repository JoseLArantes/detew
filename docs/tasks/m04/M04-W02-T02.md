## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Only permitted peer credentials and socket modes connect; missing/invalid credential lookup rejects rather than assumes root/trusted.
- [ ] Oversized/truncated/repeated frames, incompatible versions and timeouts fail within bounds and cannot force unbounded blocking/allocation.
- [ ] Privileged actions accept typed validated scope/action identifiers from an allowlist and cannot execute arbitrary shell commands or paths.
- [ ] No unauthenticated LAN service or mandatory extra account is opened; no actual live packet attach occurs as a bridge shortcut.

- Send boundary-sized, over-limit, truncated, malformed and unsupported-version control frames and stall a sender; measure bounded resources/timeouts and explicit errors.
- Attempt action/actor/path/shell metacharacter injection against fixed configd actions; compare owned files/actions and confirm no unauthorized effect.
