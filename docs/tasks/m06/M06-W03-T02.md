## Deliverables

- Earlier-of-UTC/monotonic deadline enforcement and explicit persisted UTC/restart semantics.
- Expiry reason/return-profile event and flow invalidation for temporary overrides and expiring exceptions.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] During trusted uptime, an allow expires at the earlier UTC or monotonic limit and cannot be lengthened by backward clock movement.
- [ ] After reboot, temporary allows/overrides apply only when saved UTC validity is trustworthy; uncertain time disables them with visible reason.
- [ ] Expiry affects active/idle flows and does not bypass site guardrails or clear permanent policy.

## Required tests and validation

- Controlled-clock canonical harness: create temporary profile/narrow exceptions, jump time backward/forward and cross deadlines; compare independent earliest-expiry expectations.
- Pinned native lab: reboot before/after expiry with trusted and untrusted time, while controller is unavailable; observe return profile and absence of extended allow.
- Interrupt persistence/clock restoration and present stale override state; confirm no newly extended deadline, explicit uncertainty and preserved permanent blocks.
