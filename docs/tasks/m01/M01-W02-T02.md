## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] example.org matches itself; subdomains require the explicit mode, while otherexample.org and sibling domains do not match.
- [ ] Public suffix and inappropriate wildcard/broad exception inputs are rejected under the pinned suffix rules.
- [ ] IPv4/IPv6 literals stay IP destinations; hostname exceptions do not silently authorize every service on a shared IP.
- [ ] Unicode canonical equivalents and display escaping preserve the same selector meaning across all clients.
