## Deliverables

- Safe licensed/synthetic corpus manifest with independent labels, uncertainty and protected holdout partition.
- Precision/recall/sample/uncertainty/coverage scoring specification and correction history.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The corpus includes English/Portuguese, regional/new services, IDNs, benign health/education, shared hosting and mixed-purpose examples for all required categories.
- [ ] Gold labels do not come from imported lists or current classifier output; disagreements and ambiguous labels retain review evidence.
- [ ] Eligible visible labeled destinations define precision/recall denominators; all-attempted traffic visibility/uncategorized share are separately reported.
- [ ] Holdout identity is frozen before evaluation and difficult cases cannot be removed to improve reported accuracy.

## Required tests and validation

- Have an independent reviewer label cases from taxonomy definitions without seeing provider predictions; reconcile disagreements with an auditable label-change rule.
- Calculate confusion/sample/uncertainty metrics on a hand-worked synthetic prediction table with unknown/hidden/multi-label cases; verify denominators and coverage separately.
- Audit corpus source/license/redaction fields and digest the corpus/holdout manifest before running artifact predictions.
