## Deliverables

- Versioned adult/games/ai_services definitions with English/pt-BR labels and reviewed positive/negative/mixed-purpose examples.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Adult separates sexual-content boundaries from ordinary health/education; Games distinguishes service use from incidental embedded games; AI services means primary AI-assisted function rather than any AI feature.
- [ ] IDs remain language-independent and multiple labels are permitted; an invented single primary category cannot settle policy.
- [ ] Definitions state exact/subdomain semantics and mixed-purpose/uncertain boundaries without relying on provider undocumented behavior.
- [ ] Examples are safe synthetic/curated material; no contributor must browse unsafe material or real activity.
