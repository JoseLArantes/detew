## Deliverables

- Source review inventory with exact terms/license snapshot/digest, publisher identity, attribution, formats, freshness and maintenance findings.
- Accepted/rejected/needs-permission recommendations including local-curation obligations.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Software, application signatures and category source rights are reviewed separately; public access or checksum is not permission/authenticity.
- [ ] Redistribution/modification/attribution requirements and uncertainty are stated per exact source version; missing rights leave the source unadmitted.
- [ ] Freshness/warning/expiry and offline eligibility requirements identify update cadence and credible ongoing ownership.
- [ ] The inventory preserves rejected/inadequate candidates and avoids commercial product inspiration/comparison references.

## Required tests and validation

- Read the exact primary source terms and publisher/version metadata for each candidate; independently verify interpretation and record inaccessible/ambiguous terms.
- Exercise admission with downloadable-but-unlicensed, authenticated-but-incompatible and stale/abandoned examples; record why they cannot silently become a baseline.

## Boundaries and failure behavior

- Research may proceed in parallel with M01; admitted distribution still requires accepted software/source reviews and taxonomy contracts.
- Do not download unsafe site content, submit browsing data or redistribute candidate material while rights are unresolved.
