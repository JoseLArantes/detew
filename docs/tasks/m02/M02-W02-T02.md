## Deliverables

- Owned-resource/conflict inventory and reproducible attach/detach/stop/reboot/removal experiments.
- Scoped management-exemption audit, drift handling and recovery report for each candidate.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A pre-existing conflicting capture consumer is detected before attachment and remains undisturbed; stale/drifted owned artifacts have explicit recovery.
- [ ] Stop/reboot/disable/removal restores only Detew-owned hooks/settings and preserves unrelated pf/routing/NAT configuration.
- [ ] Management exclusions are narrow, visible and audited; a destination/application outside that scope receives no hidden content bypass.
- [ ] Unclean shutdown and failed detach produce truthful degraded status and a verified console recovery procedure.

## Required tests and validation

- Take synthetic configuration/resource snapshots, introduce another capture owner and attempt attachment; compare unrelated artifacts before/after conflict rejection.
- Execute stop, reboot, disable and teardown/removal in the isolated lab while active IPv4/IPv6 flows exist; verify pf denials and unrelated rule digests survive.
- Probe documented management selectors and adjacent addresses/ports to prove the exemption boundary; simulate failed detach and recover using only the console procedure.
