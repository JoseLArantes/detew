## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Fetch outage does not instantly invalidate eligible installed data; warning/expiry thresholds and clock assumptions are explicit.
- [ ] Expired sources stop contributing labels while eligible alternatives/local corrections remain; residual gaps use insufficient classification.
- [ ] Engine enforces expiry and reconsiders active/idle flows without controller or a newly downloaded bundle.

## Required tests and validation

- Canonical eligibility fixtures and native IPv4/IPv6 lab: move one/multiple sources across warning/expiry while keeping another source/correction eligible; compare label unions and fallback reasons.

- Stop controller/network through expiry, then admit a fresh update; inspect cutoff, cached-decision invalidation, recovery and no extended stale-label use.
