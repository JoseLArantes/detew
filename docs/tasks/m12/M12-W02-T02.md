## Deliverables

- Reviewed rotation/expiry/revocation and client trust-removal instructions.
- Explicit protected CA/key backup/import procedure distinct from ordinary exports.
- Restart/upgrade/disable/uninstall trust/secret ownership and recovery behavior.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Rotation overlap/expiry and revoked scope have observed client/connection outcomes and visible readiness; no silent trust loss or broad content bypass.
- [ ] Secure key backup has explicit consent/scope/protection and authorized restore, with incompatible/unsafe backup refusal.
- [ ] Module disable/removal restores owned proxy/hooks, does not destroy unrelated trust/config, and clearly identifies remaining installed client trust to remove.
- [ ] Revocation and device trust-removal guidance distinguish supported CA/client mechanisms and verified outcomes without claiming universal remote removal.
- [ ] Private keys remain absent from ordinary diagnostics/logs/source/tests/public fixtures even during lifecycle failures.

## Required tests and validation

- Pinned native/managed-client lab with synthetic CA: rotate while connections are active, expire/revoke scope, restart/upgrade/disable/remove module and verify client trust/readiness/request outcomes.
- Authorized secure backup/import recovery on a fresh test appliance; attempt unauthorized, corrupted, incompatible or missing-key backup and confirm scoped rejection/recovery.
- Follow client trust-removal instructions on every supported device store and reattempt inspection; scan ordinary diagnostics/logs/config/history for synthetic CA key canaries.
